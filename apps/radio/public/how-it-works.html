94.7Copy03

The :15 and the :30

A matched pair written to the clock off one shared hook — 35–42 words and 70–85 words. Brand name twice in the :30, call to action last, nothing invented that wasn't on the page.

write both lengths together

Three doors: Approve → into the studio. Not this one → dropped. Suggest a change → their note goes back with the old draft, and both lengths are rewritten so they still match.

When both are approved: “Is this everything you want to record?” — add another tone, or roll.

Under the floor

The things that keep it from breaking

ResumeThe project id lives in the URL. Refresh, lose signal, close the tab — the link puts you back on the same station.
Sign-inOne shared team password. Reviewers never need it; their link carries its own token.
RuntimeEvery render is measured. Over the slot gets flagged with how many words to cut. A long read is never trimmed mid-word.
RestartsJobs interrupted by a redeploy are marked on boot and show a retry button.
PronunciationPhone numbers, URLs, prices and dates are rewritten into spoken words before the voice sees them — and shown to the client.
Music bedsPick from the library, compose one on demand, or upload a track you licensed. Composed beds are forced instrumental with an open midrange.
MasteringBed underneath, ducked by the read, mastered to −16 LUFS, padded to the exact slot.
EditingType into the script with a live words-to-clock meter. Every version is kept and restorable.
Sign-offThe reviewer gets Approve and Request changes, and the answer comes back into the project.
Outside the wizard

Three more rooms

LibraryEvery saved playlist, searchable by client, business, project or team member. Expand any project to hear the spots. Feeds the “reuse a previous playlist” shortcut back at 88.1.
Review pageWhat the client sees. Branded, read-only, one link, no password — every spot and banner with Approve and Request changes on the bottom.
DiagnosticsLive tests against every connected service, mastering, music beds and sign-in, a list of anything missing from the environment, webhook test buttons, and a rolling error log. Check it before a client ever sees a failure.
